Connect YouTube (one time, ~5 minutes, free)
  1. Open Google Cloud and create a project (any name, e.g. AutoClipper).
  2. Open YouTube Data API v3 and click Enable.
  3. Open Google Auth Platform → Get started: app name AutoClipper, your email, audience External.
  4. Go to Audience → click Publish app (so the login doesn't expire every 7 days). Google will later call it "unverified" - that's fine, it's your own app.
  5. Go to Clients → Create client → type Desktop app → Create → Download JSON.
  6. Choose that file here:
  7. Click Connect next to YouTube at the top and sign in with the Google account that owns your channel. If Google says "Google hasn't verified this app", click Advanced → Go to AutoClipper.
Connect TikTok

Click Connect next to TikTok at the top and approve AutoClipper on TikTok's page. That's all.

Advanced (optional): to use your own TikTok developer app instead of AutoClipper's, follow these steps.

  1. Open TikTok for Developers, log in and click Connect an app (create one, any name).
  2. In the app, switch to Sandbox (top of the page) and create a sandbox. Under Target users, add the TikTok account you'll post to.
  3. Under Platforms, tick Desktop.
  4. Click Add products → add Login Kit and Content Posting API.
  5. In Login Kit, add this redirect URI exactly: http://localhost:8765/oauth/tiktok/
  6. Under Scopes, make sure user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish are ticked. Save.
  7. Copy the sandbox Client key and Client secret and paste them here:
  8. Click Connect next to TikTok at the top and log in with that TikTok account.

TikTok sends each video to your TikTok inbox. Open TikTok on your phone, tap the notification, paste the caption (use the Copy button) and post. Your own app can't post publicly by itself until TikTok audits it. TikTok allows up to 5 videos waiting in your inbox per day.
